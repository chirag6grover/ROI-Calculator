How this is calculated

  • Annual manual cost = hourly rate × hours/week × working weeks/year.
  • Task volume (executions/year) = hours/week × working weeks/year — each manual hour is treated as one AI task run consuming the input/output tokens above. Adjust the token counts to match your actual workload.
  • Automation efficiency (default 80%) is the share of manual hours the automation removes; the rest stays with your team for oversight and exceptions.
  • Annual AI cost (per model) = task volume × (input tokens × input price + output tokens × output price), using cached‑input pricing for the share of tokens you mark as cached, where a model offers it.
  • Net annual savings = (manual cost × efficiency) − annual AI cost. ROI% = net savings ÷ AI cost. Payback period = AI cost ÷ daily savings rate.
  • All prices are per 1,000,000 tokens, in USD, and editable in the pricing ledger above — no provider publishes a live pricing API, so figures are a snapshot you update by hand as prices change.

This tool is for planning conversations with prospective clients, not a quote or invoice. Pricing shown reflects a June 2026 snapshot from publicly listed rates and may not include volume discounts, batch pricing, or enterprise agreements — always confirm against the provider's current pricing page before presenting final numbers.
